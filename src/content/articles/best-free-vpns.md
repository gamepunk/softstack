Most free VPNs are data-harvesting operations disguised as privacy tools. These two are the genuine exceptions.

## Proton VPN — No Logs, No Ads, No Caps

Proton VPN's free tier has no ads, no logs, and no bandwidth limits. It's run by the same Swiss nonprofit behind Proton Mail. Server selection is limited compared to paid, but for casual use on public Wi-Fi, it's all you need.

<!-- app:tor-browser -->
## Tor Browser — The Gold Standard for Anonymity

Tor routes your traffic through three random relays, making it extremely difficult to trace. Based on Firefox ESR with privacy protections built in. Not a VPN, but the strongest anonymity tool available.

## The Difference Between VPN and Tor

A VPN encrypts your traffic and routes it through one server. Tor routes through three random nodes, providing stronger anonymity at the cost of speed. VPN for everyday privacy, Tor for when you need anonymity.

## What to Avoid

Most "free" VPNs make money by selling your browsing data or injecting ads. If a VPN is free and not from a nonprofit organization, assume your data is the product.
