Being a student is expensive enough. These free apps cover everything you need for classes, papers, and exams.

<!-- app:obsidian -->
## Obsidian — Note-Taking

Store all your class notes as plain Markdown files. Link concepts across courses with backlinks. The graph view shows you how ideas connect. Free for personal use.

<!-- app:zotero -->
## Zotero — Reference Manager

Automatically save research articles from your browser with one click. Generates citations in Word, Google Docs, and LibreOffice. Free with 300MB cloud storage.

<!-- app:libreoffice -->
## LibreOffice — Office Suite

Full office suite: Writer (papers), Calc (data), Impress (presentations). Opens and saves Microsoft Office formats. No subscription.

<!-- app:notion -->
## Notion — Project Organization

Track assignments, manage group projects, and organize research in one flexible workspace. The free personal plan covers everything a student needs.
