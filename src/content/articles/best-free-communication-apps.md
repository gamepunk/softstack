The best communication tools are free, cross-platform, and don't sell your data. Here are the essential ones.

<!-- app:signal -->
## Signal — Private Messaging

Signal is the gold standard for private communication. Every message, call, and attachment is end-to-end encrypted. The protocol is so good that WhatsApp and Google Messages adopted it. It looks and works like a normal messaging app — no friction, no nerd tax.

<!-- app:discord -->
## Discord — Community Chat

Discord started as a gaming chat app and became the default for communities of all kinds. Voice channels, screen sharing, rich text chat, bots, and server organization. Free tier is genuinely generous — unlimited message history and no time limits on calls.

<!-- app:telegram -->
## Telegram — Fast & Feature-Rich

Telegram combines cloud sync that actually works with up to 2GB file sharing. Channels, bots, and group tools make it more than a messenger. Fast, free, and available everywhere.

<!-- app:zoom -->
## Zoom — Video Conferencing

Zoom earned its dominance by being the video tool that just worked. The free tier supports 40-minute group calls and unlimited 1-on-1 meetings. Virtual backgrounds, breakout rooms, and screen sharing with audio are all included.

## Which One Should You Use?

- **Private conversations** → Signal
- **Community servers** → Discord
- **Large file sharing and channels** → Telegram
- **Work meetings** → Zoom

All four are free, cross-platform, and installable via package managers.
