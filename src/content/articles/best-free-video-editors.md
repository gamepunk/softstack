You don't need Final Cut or Premiere to edit video. These free editors cover the full spectrum of video production.

<!-- app:blender -->
## Blender — The Full Package

Blender includes a complete video editor alongside its 3D tools. Multi-track timeline, keyframe animation, compositing, and color grading. Steep learning curve, unlimited power.

## DaVinci Resolve

The free version of DaVinci Resolve is Hollywood-grade. Professional color correction, Fusion VFX, Fairlight audio, and a full editing timeline. The free tier includes features most paid editors don't.

## CapCut (Desktop)

ByteDance's free editor. Intuitive timeline, auto-captions, templates, and effects. Great for social media content. Less pro-oriented than Resolve but much easier to learn.

## Quick Comparison

| Editor | Best For | Learning Curve |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| Blender | 3D + video integration | High |
| DaVinci Resolve | Professional editing | Medium |
| CapCut | Quick social content | Low |
