PC gaming doesn't require spending money. These platforms offer thousands of genuinely free games.

<!-- app:steam -->
## Steam — The Everything Store

Steam's free-to-play library is staggering: Dota 2, CS2, Warframe, Apex Legends, Destiny 2, and hundreds more. No subscription, no time limits. The client handles downloads, updates, and cloud saves.

## Epic Games Store

Epic gives away free games every week — permanently, not trials. The library of permanently-free titles is substantial if you collect them over time. Plus the same free-to-play titles as other platforms.

## Heroic Games Launcher

Open-source launcher for Epic Games and GOG. No client overhead, no storefront — just launch your games. Available on Mac, Windows, and Linux via Flatpak.

## OpenEmu (Mac)

A beautiful retro gaming frontend for macOS. Emulates consoles from Atari to PlayStation with automatic controller mapping, cover art library, and save states. Free and open-source.
