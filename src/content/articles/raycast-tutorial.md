## Why Raycast?

Raycast is more than an app launcher — it's a productivity layer on top of macOS. Open apps, run scripts, manage windows, search files, control Spotify, check your calendar, and much more without leaving the keyboard.

## Installation

```bash
brew install --cask raycast
```

Or download from [raycast.com](https://raycast.com).

## First Launch

Raycast replaces Spotlight (⌘Space). After installation, it will ask to disable the Spotlight shortcut and take over ⌘Space. Accept this — you won't miss Spotlight.

## Essential Extensions

Raycast's power comes from its extension store. Here are the must-haves:

### Window Management
Search "Window Management" in the Store. Assign hotkeys for left/right half, maximize, and more. Goodbye, mouse dragging.

### Clipboard History
Enable in Settings → Extensions → Clipboard History. Raycast saves everything you copy. Search your history and paste without touching the mouse.

### Snippets
Create text expansions for repetitive typing. For example, `@@` → your email address. Found in Settings → Extensions → Snippets.

### Quicklinks
Open frequently visited URLs with a few keystrokes. Set up in Settings → Extensions → Quicklinks. Try `gh <repo>` → `https://github.com/{query}`.

### Spotify & System Commands
Control your music, toggle Bluetooth, manage Wi-Fi, check battery — all from the command bar.

## Pro Tips

- **⌘K** in any command to see available actions
- **⌘,** to jump to Raycast settings
- Use **Floating Notes** (⌥⌘N) as a quick scratchpad
- Assign **Hotkeys** to your most-used commands for zero-keystroke access
- The **File Search** extension finds files faster than Finder

## The Learning Curve

Give it a week. The first three days you'll keep reaching for the Dock out of habit. By day seven, you'll wonder how you ever lived without it.
