PicPick combines 7 design tools into one: screen capture, image editor, color picker, palette, ruler, protractor, and whiteboard. Free for personal use.

<!-- app:picpick -->

## Screen Capture

Multiple modes: full screen, active window, region, scrolling window, freehand. The capture bar lets you quickly switch modes. Assign a hotkey for instant capture.

## Image Editor

After capture, the editor opens with a ribbon toolbar. Annotations: arrows, text, shapes, stamps, highlights. Effects: shadows, frames, reflections, watermarks. The editor supports layers for complex compositions.

## Color Picker

Click the eyedropper icon. Hover anywhere on your screen — PicPick shows the color values (RGB, HTML, C++, Delphi). Click to copy the color code. Save colors to the palette for your project.

## Pixel Ruler

Measure anything on screen in pixels, inches, or centimeters. Essential for UI design and alignment checking. The ruler is semi-transparent and always on top.

## Protractor

Overlay a semi-circular protractor on your screen. Measure angles in designs, check rotation in images. Useful for design QA and precision work.
