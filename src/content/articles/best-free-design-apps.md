You don't need a Creative Cloud subscription to do professional design work. The open-source and free software ecosystem has matured to the point where these tools rival their paid counterparts.

<!-- app:blender -->
## Blender — 3D Everything

Blender is the complete 3D pipeline: modeling, sculpting, animation, rendering, compositing, and video editing — all in one free package. It's used by indie creators and major studios. The 2.8+ overhaul brought a modern, approachable UI.

<!-- app:gimp -->
## GIMP — Image Manipulation

GIMP handles photo retouching, image composition, and graphic design. It's not Photoshop, but for the vast majority of image editing tasks, it does everything you need — layers, masks, filters, color correction, and support for every common format.

<!-- app:inkscape -->
## Inkscape — Vector Graphics

Inkscape is a professional vector graphics editor built on open standards. It uses SVG natively and includes layers, paths, text tools, gradients, and extensions. If you need vector design without Illustrator, this is it.

<!-- app:krita -->
## Krita — Digital Painting

Krita is purpose-built for digital painting, concept art, and illustration. Its brush engine rivals anything in the paid world, with stabilizers, wrap-around mode, and a resource bundle system for sharing brush packs.

## The Bottom Line

The "you need Adobe" narrative is outdated. These four tools cover 3D, raster, vector, and digital painting — all free, all open-source, all actively developed.
