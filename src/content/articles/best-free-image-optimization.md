Large images slow down websites. These free tools compress images by 30-70% with no visible quality loss.

<!-- app:imageoptim -->
## ImageOptim (Mac)

Drag and drop images to strip metadata, compress PNGs and JPEGs, and optimize for web. The gold standard for Mac image compression. Free and open-source.

## Squoosh (Web)

Google's web-based image compressor at squoosh.app. Compares original and compressed side-by-side. Supports WebP, AVIF, MozJPEG, and more. No install needed.

## TinyPNG (Web)

The classic online compressor at tinypng.com. Free for up to 20 images at a time, 5MB each. Excellent PNG and JPEG compression with a simple drag-and-drop interface.

## When to Use Which

| Tool | Best For | Platform |
|------|----------|----------|
| ImageOptim | Bulk local optimization | Mac |
| Squoosh | Quality comparison | Web |
| TinyPNG | Quick one-offs | Web |
