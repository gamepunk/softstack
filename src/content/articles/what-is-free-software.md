"Free software" means different things to different people. Here's a clear breakdown of the categories you'll encounter on SoftStack.

## Free (Gratis)

Costs zero dollars. No payment required to use all features. Examples: Discord, Spotify (free tier), Zoom (free tier). The software may or may not be open-source.

## Open Source

The source code is publicly available. You can inspect it, modify it, and redistribute it. Usually also free, but not always. Examples: VS Code, Blender, Firefox, Linux.

The terms "open source" and "free software" (as in freedom) overlap but come from different philosophical traditions. Open source emphasizes the practical benefits; free software emphasizes user freedom.

## Freemium

A free tier with paid upgrades. The free tier should be genuinely useful on its own — not a crippled demo. Examples: Notion, Figma, Bitwarden, Linear.

SoftStack only recommends freemium apps where the free tier is genuinely useful without paying.

## Free Trial

Temporarily free, then requires payment. SoftStack does NOT include free trials. If an app requires payment after 7 or 30 days, it's not free.

## What SoftStack Means by "Free"

On this site, "free" means you can use the app indefinitely without paying. It might be open-source, ad-supported, or freemium with a generous free tier — but there's no clock ticking toward a paywall.
