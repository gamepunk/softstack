A handpicked collection of free tools that turn macOS into a serious development machine. Every app here is genuinely free or open-source, actively maintained, and installable with a single command.

## 1. Visual Studio Code

<!-- app:visual-studio-code -->

The editor that ate the world. VS Code combines a lightweight feel with IDE-level power through its extension marketplace. Built-in Git, terminal, debugger, and remote development make it a complete toolkit.

## 2. iTerm2

<!-- app:iterm2 -->

The terminal macOS should have shipped with. Split panes, instant replay, powerful search, and deep customization. The tmux integration alone makes it worth switching from Terminal.app.

## 3. Rectangle

<!-- app:rectangle -->

macOS window management is broken. Rectangle fixes it with keyboard shortcuts for snapping windows to halves, thirds, and quarters. Free, open-source, zero configuration.

## 4. Maccy

<!-- app:maccy -->

A lightweight clipboard manager that lives in your menu bar. Everything you copy is saved and searchable. Press a shortcut, type what you're looking for, and paste.

## 5. Obsidian

<!-- app:obsidian -->

Your second brain, in plain Markdown files you own forever. Backlinks, graph view, and 1,000+ community plugins. Perfect for technical notes and documentation.
