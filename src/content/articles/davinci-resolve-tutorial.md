DaVinci Resolve is Hollywood-grade software that's completely free. Here's how to get from zero to your first export.

<!-- app:davinci-resolve -->

## The Interface

Resolve has dedicated workspaces at the bottom: Media, Cut, Edit, Fusion, Color, Fairlight, and Deliver. For basic editing, you'll mostly use the Edit page. The timeline is the large area at the bottom.

## Importing Media

1. Switch to the **Media** page
2. Navigate to your footage in the file browser
3. Drag clips into the media pool (the bin area)
4. Switch to the **Edit** page

## Basic Editing

Drag clips from the media pool onto the timeline. The toolbar above the timeline has your essential tools:

- **Blade (B)** — Cut a clip into two pieces
- **Trim (T)** — Drag the edge of a clip to shorten or lengthen
- **Selection (A)** — Move clips around

Select unwanted sections and press Delete. Drag clips to rearrange them in the timeline. Add more clips to build your sequence.

## Transitions

Drag the edge of two overlapping clips and choose a transition from the popup, or go to Effects Library → Video Transitions and drag one between two clips. Cross Dissolve is the standard.

## Color Grading

Switch to the **Color** page. The primary wheels control lift (shadows), gamma (midtones), and gain (highlights). Drag the center dot in each wheel to shift colors. Use the curves below for fine control. Apply a LUT from the LUT browser for a quick look.

## Export

Switch to **Deliver**. Choose H.264 for web, H.265 for smaller files. Set resolution (1920x1080 for Full HD). Pick a destination and click "Add to Render Queue" then "Render All."

For YouTube, export at 1080p or higher, 24 or 30 fps, H.264 at 10-20 Mbps. That's all you need.
