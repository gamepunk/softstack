Microsoft PowerToys packs 20+ utilities into one app. Here are the ones that will change how you use Windows.

<!-- app:powertoys -->

## FancyZones — Window Manager

The killer feature. Press Win+Shift+` to open the zone editor. Create custom window layouts — columns, rows, grids, priority layouts. Hold Shift while dragging a window to snap it into zones. No more manually resizing windows.

## PowerRename

Right-click any files in Explorer → PowerRename. Search and replace text in filenames with regex support, case sensitivity, and preview. Rename hundreds of files in seconds.

## Keyboard Manager

Remap any key to any other key or shortcut. Swap Caps Lock and Escape. Make Ctrl+Shift+E send whatever you want. Useful for non-standard keyboards or muscle memory from other OSes.

## Image Resizer

Right-click images → Resize pictures. Choose from presets (Small, Medium, Large) or custom dimensions. Batch resize photos for email, web, or documents in one click.

## Text Extractor

Win+Shift+T activates OCR. Select a region of your screen and the text is extracted to your clipboard. Works on images, PDFs, and anything displayed on screen.

## Color Picker

Win+Shift+C activates the system-wide color picker. Click anywhere on screen to copy the color as HEX, RGB, or HSL. Essential for designers and developers.

## PowerToys Run

Alt+Space opens a Spotlight-like launcher. Search files, run commands, calculate, and more. A faster alternative to the Start Menu search.
