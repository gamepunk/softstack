Your browser is your most-used app. Here's how the top free browsers compare on privacy, speed, and features.

<!-- app:firefox -->
## Firefox — The Independent Choice

Firefox is the only major browser not built on Chromium and not controlled by an ad company. Enhanced Tracking Protection blocks trackers and fingerprinting. Multi-Account Containers let you isolate work, personal, and shopping identities.

**Best for:** Privacy-conscious users who want independence from Google's browser engine.

<!-- app:brave-browser -->
## Brave — Privacy by Default

Brave blocks ads and trackers out of the box, which makes pages load significantly faster than Chrome. It's Chromium-based so all extensions work. Optional crypto rewards, built-in Tor private tabs, and IPFS support.

**Best for:** Speed and privacy without sacrificing Chrome compatibility.

<!-- app:vivaldi -->
## Vivaldi — The Power User's Browser

Vivaldi is built on Chromium but runs circles around Chrome in features: tab stacking, tiling, sidebar panels, mouse gestures, CSS-level themes. Based in Norway with end-to-end encrypted sync.

**Best for:** Users who want maximum customization and control.

<!-- app:chromium -->
## Chromium — Chrome Without Google

Chromium is the open-source project Chrome is built on. Same rendering, same extensions, no Google tracking. Updates are community-maintained rather than automatic.

**Best for:** Developers who need Chrome's engine without Google's telemetry.
