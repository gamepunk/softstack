Warp is a terminal emulator built from the ground up in Rust, with features that make command-line work feel like a modern IDE: block-based output, cursor positioning for editing commands, built-in AI for natural-language-to-command translation, and a team collaboration layer.

The terminal has been essentially unchanged for 40 years. Warp is the first serious attempt to rethink it, and it delivers — especially for developers who spend half their day in the command line.
